      Who decides what

      Universities decide admission. Embassies decide visas. Employers decide jobs. A consultancy prepares and submits your file but cannot grant any of these.

      How consultancies earn

      Most are free to students because partner universities pay them 10–20% of first-year tuition. That is why they push partner universities. Top US schools and German public universities pay no commission, so consultancies charge a service fee for them.

      The visa-enabling document

      Every visa hangs on one document from the university: I-20 (USA), CAS (UK), CoE (Australia), LOA + PAL (Canada), admission letter (Germany). No document, no visa application.

      How a genuine job abroad works

      A real overseas job comes from an employer who sponsors your work visa. Anyone in India who recruits for jobs abroad must hold a Recruiting Agent licence from the Ministry of External Affairs, listed on eMigrate.

      Licensed recruiting agent: the steps

        Walk away if you see these

        • No Recruiting Agent licence number, or it does not match the eMigrate listing.
        • "Guaranteed job", "visa in 15 days", or an offer without any interview with the employer.
        • Fees above the legal cap, cash-only payment, or no receipt.
        • Travel on a tourist visa "to convert to a work visa later". This is the usual route into trafficking and scam compounds.
        • A contract in a language you cannot read, or someone holding your passport.

        Complaints: eMigrate portal, or the Overseas Workers Resource Centre helpline 1800-113-090 (24×7), per MEA.

        Documents for most work visas

        Passport, signed offer and contract, degree and transcripts (often attested or apostilled), experience letters with payslips, skills assessment or professional licence, police clearance certificate, medical exam, English test where required (IELTS General, OET for nurses), CV and photos.
